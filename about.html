About Us

WCAA — New Jersey & Southeastern Pennsylvania Chapter

We are proud members of the Window Coverings Association of America, the only national non-profit trade association dedicated to the retail window coverings industry and to the dealers, designers, decorators, workrooms, fabricators, and installers that are our members. We meet monthly — typically on a Thursday morning — and meetings are announced via email, our Facebook page, Instagram, and the Events page.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to make available educational opportunities in the window coverings and interior fashions professions, to encourage a code of ethics for fair business practice in the industry, and to work for the betterment of the industry.

Our Goals

  • To promote professionalism in the industry
  • To provide a common voice for the interests of independent retailers
  • To aid in the success and profitability of our members
